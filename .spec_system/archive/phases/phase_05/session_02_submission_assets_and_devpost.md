# Session 02: Submission Assets and Devpost

**Session ID**: `phase05-session02-submission-assets-and-devpost`
**Status**: Complete
**Estimated Tasks**: ~20-25
**Estimated Duration**: 2-4 hours

---

## Objective

Turn the tested release and redacted sample into one concise judge package,
prepare a narrated product demo, capture the Codex feedback reference,
prepare every repository-safe Education-category Devpost field, and provide
one exact handoff for the human who owns external publishing and submission.

---

## Scope

### In Scope (MVP)

- A practical submission checklist backed by the repository's existing
  product, release, evidence, distribution, image, and runtime checks.
- Judge-ready root README covering sample input, setup/run path, architecture,
  AI/Codex usage, GPT-5.6/Tavily runtime, tests, privacy, limits, and license.
- Curated deterministic sample plus redacted live result evidence suitable for
  screenshots and the video without exposing credentials or learner data.
- Repository license, private reviewer-access instructions, intended release
  identity, and judge reproduction verification.
- Primary Codex `/feedback` Session ID capture and repository-safe record.
- Under-three-minute storyboard, narration, screen capture, edit, verified
  private upload candidate, and complete YouTube upload metadata.
- Education-category Devpost title, short/long description, problem, solution,
  technical implementation, OpenAI usage, challenges, accomplishments,
  learning, next steps, links, media, credits, and required acknowledgements.
- Exact local commit, intended tag, media, and evidence reconciliation plus a
  concise human GitHub, YouTube, and Devpost handoff.
- Final existing release, distribution, production-image, health, persistence,
  privacy, and secret checks after all tracked assets are complete.

### Out of Scope

- Product feature work, hosted deployment, P1 roadmap work, or edits that make
  the video diverge from the tested release.
- Submission-specific runtime validators, parallel release frameworks, or
  contract tests that duplicate established release checks.
- Agent-operated repository access changes, reviewer invitations, branch
  pushes, tag creation/push, YouTube authentication/upload/publication,
  Devpost mutation/submission, or platform receipts.
- Publishing secrets, private credentials, real learner content, provider
  payloads, hidden reasoning, local paths, or unrestricted artifact links.
- Inventing a legal basis, regulatory-compliance claim, provider deletion
  guarantee, CODEOWNERS identity, or security reporting contact.
