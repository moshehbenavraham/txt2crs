# Session 02: Submission Assets and Devpost

**Session ID**: `phase05-session02-submission-assets-and-devpost`
**Status**: Not Started
**Estimated Tasks**: ~20-25
**Estimated Duration**: 2-4 hours

---

## Objective

Turn the tested release and redacted sample into one concise judge package,
publish the narrated product demo, capture the Codex feedback reference,
complete every Education-category Devpost field, and preserve confirmation
that the exact release was submitted before the deadline.

---

## Scope

### In Scope (MVP)

- Tests-first submission checklist and deterministic validation for required
  local files, links, version/tag/revision identity, and public-safe content.
- Judge-ready root README covering sample input, setup/run path, architecture,
  AI/Codex usage, GPT-5.6/Tavily runtime, tests, privacy, limits, and license.
- Curated deterministic sample plus redacted live result evidence suitable for
  screenshots and the video without exposing credentials or learner data.
- Repository license, visibility/access, release tag, and judge reproduction
  verification.
- Primary Codex `/feedback` Session ID capture and repository-safe record.
- Under-three-minute storyboard, narration, screen capture, edit, public
  YouTube publication, and final link verification.
- Education-category Devpost title, short/long description, problem, solution,
  technical implementation, OpenAI usage, challenges, accomplishments,
  learning, next steps, links, media, credits, and required acknowledgements.
- Final submission dry run, exact commit/tag/link reconciliation, submission,
  timestamped receipt, and post-submit verification.

### Out of Scope

- Product feature work, hosted deployment, P1 roadmap work, or edits that make
  the video diverge from the tested release.
- Publishing secrets, private credentials, real learner content, provider
  payloads, hidden reasoning, local paths, or unrestricted artifact links.
- Inventing a legal basis, regulatory-compliance claim, provider deletion
  guarantee, CODEOWNERS identity, or security reporting contact.

---

4. Devpost submission receipt tying the deadline, repository, release tag,
   video, and tested revision together.
